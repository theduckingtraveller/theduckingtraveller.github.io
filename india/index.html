Kerala Backwaters

Experience the tranquil beauty of Kerala's palm-lined backwaters, houseboats, and lush landscapes—a true escape into nature's lap.

Jaipur

The Pink City dazzles with its royal palaces, vibrant bazaars, and rich Rajasthani culture—perfect for history and architecture lovers.

Guwahati and Shillong

Discover the gateway to Northeast India with Guwahati's temples and Shillong's rolling hills, waterfalls, and colonial charm.

Ranthambore National Forest

Home to majestic tigers, Ranthambore offers thrilling safaris, ancient ruins, and a wild adventure in Rajasthan's heart.

Kaziranga National Park and Cherrapunji Forest

Witness the one-horned rhino in Kaziranga and explore the lush, rain-soaked wonders of Cherrapunji's forests and caves.

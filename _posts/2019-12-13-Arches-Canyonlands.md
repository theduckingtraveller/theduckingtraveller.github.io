## <a id="getting-here"></a> Getting Here

The best way to get here is to fly into either Salt Lake City or Las Vegas and drive to Moab. It is a 4 hour drive from Salt Lake City and an 6 hour drive from Las Vegas. Renting a car from either of the cities and driving in is the best option in my opinion.

Make sure you have your park pass and read about any closures and restrictions [here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/national-park-system-operating-status.htm)

## <a id="weather"></a> Best Time to Travel

As I say about most places, best time to visit is during shoulder seasons. Summers here are too hot and winters are too cold. The sweet spot is 
April-May and September-November (just before the snow starts to fall)

## <a id="day1"></a> Day 1 Canyonlands National Park


[Map of the Canyonlands National Park](https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/maps.htm)

You can use this consolidated view on google maps for your day 1 itinerary [Map for Day 1](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b3hLjazhVq8Ejaeq7) 

Both Arches and Canyonlands are such unique national parks with so many arches as the name suggests. The park has a very distinct, colorful landscape and unique but dramatic scenery. The park is a 10 minute drive if you are staying in Moab. Grab coffee, a bit to eat and make your way into the park early.
